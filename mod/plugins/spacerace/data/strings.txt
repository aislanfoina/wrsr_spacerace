591000	Rocket Research Institute (NII-88)
591001	The captured V-2 is studied, copied as the R-1 and outgrown. A research institute for long-range rockets opens the space branch of the tree.
591002	Liquid Rocket Engines
591003	Turbopump-fed kerosene and liquid oxygen engines. Unlocks the Rocket Engine Works and the Engine Test Stand: every engine is fired once before it flies.
591004	Cryogenic Oxygen
591005	Air separation on an industrial scale. Unlocks the Oxygen-Nitrogen Plant. Liquid oxygen boils away: produce it close to the pad.
591006	Radio Guidance and Telemetry
591007	Gyroscopes, radio control and telemetry. Unlocks the Instrument Works.
591008	The R-7 Semyorka
591009	Korolev's two-stage intercontinental rocket: a core and four strap-on boosters, twenty engines lit on the ground. Unlocks the Rocket Plant, the MIK and the R-7 Launch Complex.
591010	Artificial Earth Satellite
591011	A simple satellite before the Americans fly theirs. Unlocks the Spacecraft Assembly Hall, the Deep Space Tracking Station and the Sputnik mission.
591012	Biological Satellites
591013	Life support in orbit and a way back: dogs first. Unlocks the Landing and Recovery Field.
591014	Lunar Probes
591015	The Blok E upper stage reaches escape velocity. Luna probes hit the Moon and photograph its far side.
591016	Manned Spaceflight
591017	The Vostok spacecraft and the cosmonaut corps. Unlocks the Cosmonaut Training Centre: graduates who train there become experts.
591018	Glory to the Conquerors of Space
591019	Monuments to the first man in space and to those who follow.
591020	Storable Propellants
591021	UDMH and nitrogen tetroxide ignite on contact and keep for months. Toxic and dangerous. Unlocks the Propellant Plant.
591022	Extravehicular Activity
591023	An inflatable airlock and a pressure suit that has to shrink back through it.
591024	The Soyuz Spacecraft
591025	Three cosmonauts, rendezvous and docking. Unlocks the Soyuz rocket.
591026	Heavy Launcher (UR-500)
591027	Chelomei's hypergolic heavy rocket. Unlocks the Proton for Zond and the lunar probes.
591028	The N1 Moon Rocket
591029	Thirty engines in the first stage, 105 m, 2750 t. Unlocks the Heavy Launch Complex. Without the NK-33 engines and a tested first stage it will fail.
591030	NK-33 Engines and KORD
591031	Kuznetsov's reusable engines and a control system that shuts down a failing engine and its opposite. Makes the N1 far more likely to survive its first stage.
591032	Lunar Landing (L3)
591033	The LK lander and the LOK orbiter. With these the N1 carries a cosmonaut to the Moon.
